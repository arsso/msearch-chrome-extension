Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

msearch does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to the developer or to any third party.

  • Tabs, browsing history, bookmarks and recently closed tabs are read locally on your device only to display matching entries in the palette. They never leave your browser.
  • Settings (search engine, suggestions on or off) are stored with Chrome's sync storage, which is tied to your own Google account and is not accessible to the developer.
  • Search suggestions. When suggestions are enabled (on by default, can be turned off in the options page), the text you type in the palette is sent to Google's suggestion service at suggestqueries.google.com to show completions. No other identifier is sent with it.
  • Web search. When you run a web search from the palette, the query is sent to the search engine you selected in the options page.
  • The extension contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code and does not read or modify the content of the pages you visit.

Contact: arssolabs@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/arsso/msearch-chrome-extension.
